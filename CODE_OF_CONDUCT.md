# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically specific.
- Welcome good-faith questions and differing viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, and ideas rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
